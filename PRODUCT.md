# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

A person keeping today's habit list on the phone or the installed site. A partner, after a confirmed pairing, sees only Kept or Not yet.

## Product Purpose

Habuilt is the list for today: mark a habit kept, set a reminder, review the week on Sunday, and spend points in a personal reward shop. Success is the same list on the next session, with past check-in points left as they were stored.

## Positioning

The checklist is the saved habit definitions. Check-ins are a separate ledger. The phone does not invent a second score for sleep, HRV, or vitality.

## Operating Context

The daily path is Today, Habits, Rewards, and Review. Focus and Insights stay in Account. Reminders use the saved start time. Guests stay on the device until they sign in. Export is the account file.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Habit definitions are name, schedule, time, points, category, and hint. Personal Sunday notes stay on the review. Points on an old check-in are not rewritten when a habit's price changes. An unknown habit id is rejected and the tick comes off the phone. No email, SMS, or push subscription. The Android app is a wrapper of this site. Do not invent prices, testimonials, customer counts, or health measurements.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Habuilt. The ground is #F7F8F2, body and button text is #0C110D, and #D9F27A is a fill only. Titles use Urbanist. The interface uses Inter. The voice is a calm habit tracker. Do not use warrior, executive, or vitality marketing, and do not use the Peridot name.

## Evidence on Hand

The live screens, the saved-list and follow-up tests, and the founder's locked palette and type. No testimonials, prices, or case studies are on hand. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Today's list is the product. Summaries point back to the checklist.
- The server owns check-in points. The phone shows what was stored.
- A partner sees Kept or Not yet, not the habit names.
- Say what the control does, in sentence case.
- One visual system on every screen of the daily path.
