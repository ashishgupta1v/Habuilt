The Principle

Why 1% daily changes everything

James Clear's core insight: habits that improve 1% daily compound to 37× over a year. But the mechanism isn't magic — it's progressive overload applied to behaviour. Every habit below has four tiers. You start at Tier 1 (the floor) and graduate when you can do it without thinking.

The four-tier system

Tier 1 · Floor — The minimum viable version. You can do this on your worst day, exhausted, with a sick baby. Never miss Tier 1 twice.

Tier 2 · Foundation — The 80% version. Weeks 1–6. You're building the groove.

Tier 3 · Standard — The full habit as designed. Weeks 7–14. This is what the household plan describes.

Tier 4 · Mastery — The optimised version. Weeks 15–26. You've earned the right to push.

Progressive Timeline

26 weeks, four phases

Start at the floor. Graduate each habit when it's automatic. By week 26, you're running the full system without thinking — that's what atomic habits means.

Weeks 1–2 · Floor

Tier 1 Everything

Every habit at its minimum. Wake 05:30. Deep Block 30 min. 10 min stretch. 2 touches/day. The only goal: don't miss two days in a row. Build the streaks.

Weeks 3–6 · Foundation

Tier 2 Core Habits

Morning system → Tier 2. Wake 05:15. Deep Block 1h. Boxing 15 min. 3 touches/day. Evening and nutrition stay Tier 1. Graduate habits one at a time, not all at once.

Weeks 7–14 · Standard

Tier 3 Full System

This is the household plan as written. 05:00 wake. 1h30 deep block. 30 min boxing. 5 touches/day. Full evening routine. All nutrition protocols. First contracts landing.

Weeks 15–26 · Mastery

Tier 4 Optimised

Everything automatic. 04:45 travel days. 1h45 deep block. 30 min + 5K. 5 touches + warm follow-ups. Financial dashboard. ₹9L/month run-rate territory.
