## 6. Postpartum, Lactation & Clinical Health Protocols

### 1. Serum B12 (245 pg/mL) & MCV (101 fL) Protocol
- **Clinical Rationale:** Serum B12 of 245 pg/mL is borderline deficient. Elevated MCV (101 fL) indicates early macrocytosis. Breastmilk cobalamin is directly dependent on maternal intake; B12 is essential for infant myelin sheath development.
- **Intervention:** 1000 mcg Sublingual Methylcobalamin daily on waking (empty stomach). Sublingual absorption directly enters systemic circulation, bypassing gastrointestinal absorption limits.

### 2. Vitamin D3 (20.63 ng/mL) Repletion Protocol
- **Clinical Rationale:** 20.63 ng/mL is clinically insufficient (optimal postpartum range is 40–60 ng/mL). Maternal Vitamin D deficiency impairs bone mineral density, immunity, and breastmilk vitamin D content.
- **Intervention:**
  1. Weekly high-dose Vitamin D3 sachet taken every Sunday with fatty lunch (8-week repletion phase).
  2. Daily Vitamin D3 maintenance drops + vegetarian Algal DHA taken with breakfast.

### 3. Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP: 11 U/L) Reversal Protocol
- **Clinical Rationale:** ALP at 11 U/L is markedly low (normal range: 30–120 U/L). In postpartum women, low ALP typically indicates zinc deficiency, magnesium deficiency, or subclinical thyroid sluggishness.
- **Intervention:**
  - **Targeted Zinc & Folate Foods:** Soaked pumpkin seeds, white sesame seeds (til), cashews, soaked legumes (chana/rajma), and steamed spinach.
  - **Night Magnesium Glycinate:** 200–300mg elemental magnesium in warm milk at 21:15 to support enzymatic cofactors and deep sleep.

### 4. Normal Iron (89 µg/dL) & Hemoglobin (13.1 g/dL) Protection
- **Clinical Rationale:** Hemoglobin of 13.1 g/dL and Iron of 89 µg/dL demonstrate optimal baseline erythropoiesis. *No synthetic iron supplements are needed*, avoiding gut distress and constipation.
- **The Tea Rule:** Never consume tea or coffee within 1 hour before or after any meal. Polyphenols and tannins chelate dietary iron and impair absorption.

### 5. 3.5L Lactation Hydration & Physical Recovery
- **Hydration:** 3.5 Litres daily tracked. Keep a marked 1L bottle at the nursing station. Prevents dehydration-induced milk supply drop and maternal headaches.
